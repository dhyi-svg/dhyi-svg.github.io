I'm a sophomore at Carnegie Mellon studying Statistics and Machine Learning, with minors in Robotics and Biomedical Engineering. I do assistive robotics research at the Robotics Institute, advised by [Dr. Zackory Erickson](https://zackory.com/) and [Dr. Henny Admoni](https://hennyadmoni.com/).

I build autonomous manipulation systems for wheelchair-mounted robot arms — perception, calibration, and real-time control that let an arm do something useful for someone who needs it. My current project is part of a bigger autonomous feeding demo, building on a water-bottle pick-and-handoff pipeline I developed over the past year.

The problem I keep coming back to is intent: how a machine figures out what a person wants from signals that are noisy and ambiguous, and how it assists without taking over. I'm starting work on eye-gaze intent inference, and longer term I want to build prosthetics and neural interfaces.

## Research interests

- Assistive manipulation and shared autonomy
- Human intent inference from gaze and biosignals
- Real-time perception and control on constrained hardware
- Prosthetics and neural interfaces
